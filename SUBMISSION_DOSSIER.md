## SECTION 05: Short Public Summary & Post-Mortem

### The Problem
Autonomous AI agents are being granted authority to execute real financial transfers and clear treasury balances across Ethereum, Sepolia, and DeFi protocols. However, an agent prompted with a forged invoice, a sanctioned exploiter address, or floating-point rounded math can drain organizational treasuries in seconds. 

### The Solution: Praman Desk
Praman Desk serves as an autonomous pre-payment spend guard and clearing desk for AI agents on Telegraph Protocol:
1. **Pre-Payment Verification Gate**: Before signing an on-chain transfer, an agent passes the proposed counterparty and invoice through Praman Desk.
2. **Primary Counterparty Registries**: Queries live statutory registers (GLEIF Global LEI, German Handelsregister, SEC EDGAR, VIES EU VAT) in real time with zero third-party API keys required.
3. **Exploit & Sanctions Defense (Mission 02)**: Screens recipient EVM addresses against 19,565 US Treasury OFAC SDN entries and known exploiters (such as the Lazarus Group Ronin Bridge exploit). When transactions exceed $1,000, mandatory FATF Travel Rule verification is enforced.
4. **Deterministic WebAssembly Engine (Mission 04)**: A 651-byte zero-dependency WASM comparator performs bit-exact 3-way reconciliation (Invoice vs Purchase Order vs Goods Receipt), eliminating floating-point rounding errors and flagging single-cent discrepancies.
5. **Telegraph x402 Micropayments**: Purchases verification claims from paid Telegraph miners over x402 payment channels, logging immutable settlement receipts on Ethereum Sepolia.

### Honest Post-Mortem & Known Limitations
- **Synthetic Token Shortcut Failure (Adapter #461 Post-Mortem)**: During early evaluator training, an initial model scored 46.7% on Devnode due to a token frequency shortcut (Russian entity name tokens vs narcotics tags). We addressed this in dataset v4 through pairwise symmetric coupling (every clean entity paired with an identical-length SDN match), reducing surface probe scores to 63.7% (below the 65% ceiling) and retraining ModernBERT-base LoRA to 98.67% local validation accuracy.
- **Network Boundaries**: Real statutory queries (GLEIF, SEC EDGAR) are subject to public rate limits; Praman Desk uses cached statutory mirrors and Telegraph miner fallbacks when upstream endpoints experience transient latency.
- **Gas & Settlement Costs**: Every paid x402 verification costs $0.01 test USDC on Ethereum Sepolia, providing micro-economic security without prohibitive gas friction.
